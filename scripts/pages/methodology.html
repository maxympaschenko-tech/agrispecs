Tractors and implements vary by model year, market, engine, transmission and optional equipment. A model name alone does not identify every configuration.

Sources

We prioritize manufacturer operator manuals, official brochures, published specifications and OEM parts catalogs. Dealer literature and owner reports can provide context but are distinguished from official data.

Verification principles

  • Identify the precise model and relevant configuration.
  • Distinguish engine horsepower from PTO horsepower.
  • Distinguish loader lift capacity at the pivot pin from other measuring points.
  • Show units explicitly, including US gallons versus imperial gallons.
  • Keep unknown facts unreported rather than guessing.
  • Do not assert replacement part compatibility without evidence.

Safety and limitations

For repairs, safety procedures and operating loads, consult the manufacturer documentation for your exact machine and serial number. Our content is reference information, not a substitute for a qualified mechanic or the official service manual.
