Farm Machine Specs aims to publish useful answers to real owner questions, grounded in checkable sources instead of generic, automatically repeated pages.

Our editorial standards

  • Describe which model year and configuration a technical claim applies to.
  • Prioritize original manufacturer documents and label anecdotal observations.
  • Never invent dimensions, capacities, tests, reviews, prices or parts interchangeability.
  • Explain meaningful safety risks and when a technician is required.
  • Correct published errors when stronger evidence becomes available.
  • Clearly distinguish advertising and affiliate relationships from independent technical facts.

AI-assisted work

Digital tools, including AI, may assist with drafts and organization. They do not serve as technical evidence; critical claims require independent source verification before publication.
