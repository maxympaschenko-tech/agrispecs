# Organic-growth plan — US market

**Core positioning**: "The practical tractor & farm machinery reference: verified specs, maintenance, troubleshooting, parts and fitment." Do not try to out-count TractorData's model inventory.

## Publication priority
1. Specific repairs: "Kubota BX23S won't start", "John Deere 1025R PTO not engaging", "New Holland Workmaster hydraulic lift slow". Diagnose by symptom with inspection steps and safety warnings; cite manuals.
2. Model service references: "Kubota L2501 engine oil capacity", "John Deere 5075E hydraulic oil type", maintenance interval charts with year/configuration caveats.
3. Fitment and parts: "Does [attachment] fit [tractor]?", filters, belts, fluid specs, serial-number-aware references. Cite OEM parts catalogs; never assert interchange without evidence.
4. Model comparisons with decision tables: Kubota L2501 vs L3301, Deere 1025R vs Kubota BX2380. Compare matched configurations and explain limits.
5. Buying advice with situation-based answers: "best tractor for 5 acres", "compact vs subcompact", "what size brush hog for 25 PTO hp", used-tractor inspection checklist.
6. Evergreen tools: lbs↔kg, in↔mm, hp↔kW, US gal↔L, compatible hitch/PTO check (later once sourced records exist).
7. Equipment expansion: skid steers, combines, balers, mowers, tillers — after traction in core compact/utility tractor clusters.

## Editorial quality gate
- Distinct user intent; not thin spun model pages.
- Confirm exact model year, engine, drive type and implement variant.
- Show imperial US units first and metric equivalents where appropriate.
- Identify first-party source, source checked date and unresolved contradictions.
- Repair content includes risks, prechecks, diagnostic flow, when to seek a technician.
- Use original/licensed images; never invent an owner's review or laboratory test.
- Link model ⇄ troubleshooting ⇄ maintenance ⇄ compatible equipment ⇄ comparison.
- Recheck changed manufacturer information periodically.

## Minimum launch
Homepage, complete taxonomy/navigation, 15–25 source-backed model pages, 15 deep troubleshooting articles, 10 maintenance references, 5 curated comparisons, methodology, editorial policy and legal pages. These are **targets**, not content already published.

## Search and monetization
Prioritize query usefulness and strong internal links, not mass autogenerated pages. Search Console query data determines expansion. AdSense only after sufficient unique useful pages; keep ads out of repair step sequences and important spec tables.
