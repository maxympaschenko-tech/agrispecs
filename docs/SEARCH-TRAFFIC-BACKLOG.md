## Publishing discipline
Per page: clear answer first; exact configuration; original/source-permitted images; real tables and numbered inspection flow; honest "unknown" fields; cited source(s), last verified, relevant internal links, FAQ section only when substantively useful. Do not fabricate tests, prices or interchangeability.
